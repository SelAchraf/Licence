TRIP PROGRAM

PRICE: 180.000 Da
DEPARTURE: June 5 at 8 a.m at rabeh bitat airport
ARRIVAL: June 5 at 12 a.m Istanbul airport
HOTEL: The Marmara Taksim hotel
THE PROGRAM:
FIRST DAY: you can't go to Türkiye without having a nice Turkish food experience so Bosphorus Strait will be our destination Where can you enjoy the strait breeze while sipping cup of Turkish Coffee and eat baklava or Knafeh.
SECOND DAY: We will delve into the ancient Ottoman civilization by visiting the hagia sophia Mosque and the blue mosque.
THIRD DAY: Turkey is famous for its spices, and it has its own bazaar in Istanbul (spice bazaar ), where our next destination is a meeting place for food lovers.
FOURTH DAY: topkapi palace will be Our next stop It is considered the embodiment of Ottoman architecture, thanks to Mimar Sinan Who gave all his capabilities to this masterpiece.
FIFTH DAY: our destination is antalya Where will sea lovers be dazzled by the splendor of its sea.
And at least We will end our trip with some adventure as we will go on a trip in the skies of Antalya with the zeppelin tour.
