Get picked up from Krakow's city center for an easy journey to the UNESCO World Heritage Site, where you'll descend deep underground to explore a vast network of historic mining tunnels, salt lakes and sculptures of intricate salt carved over the centuries.

FIFTH DAY: Bialowieza Forest is a highly preserved UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is known to be the only virgin forest in Europe, extending over more than 3000 square kilometers from the Polish eastern border to the western Belarusian border, straddling the two where we will spend our day.
