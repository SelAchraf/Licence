About US

Bona Tours is a travel agency located in ANNABA, run by student SELOUGHA Mohamed Achraf, with the help of his friend SELAIMIA Akrem.
Bona Tours has a primary objective which is to satisfy you wherever you are by providing you with what you need so that you are satisfied during your trip.
Our agency makes organized trips at national and international level, allowing you to get to know tourists who come from all over the country or the world, which will allow you to exchange and expand your knowledge and your culture as well as building new relationships.
And don't forget the facilities that our agency offers you in choosing the best hotels for your trip.
The agency is open 6 days a week, from Saturday to Thursday, from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., to make you a privileged person.
