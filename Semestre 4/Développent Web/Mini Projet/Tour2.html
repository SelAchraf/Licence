TRIP PROGRAM

PRICE: 60.000 Da
DEPARTURE: June 5 at 9 a.m In front of the agency's headquarters ARRIVAL: June 5 at 17 p.m at Movenpick Hotel HOTEL: Movenpick Hotel Du Lac Tunis THE PROGRAM : FIRST DAY: We will get to know the Roman civilization in Tunisia by visiting the ruins of dougga and classified From within world heritage list of Unesco.
SECOND DAY: we will go to Bardo National Museum which is a jewel of Tunisian heritage.
THIRD DAY: We will have a little fun by visiting carthage Land to entertain the kids (and even the adults).
FOURTH DAY: Famous for its cobbled streets and blue and white houses Sidi Bou Saïd will be our next stop wher u can see its ancient port And taste traditional Tunisian food.
FIFTH DAY: our destination is el hammamet wich is a City that u should visit in Tunisia and it is the time of music and theater festivals, not to be missed under any circumstances.
And at least We will end our trip Visit the island of Djerba ,its magnificent beaches and picturesque villages characterized by the typical square houses is considered one of the best regions of Tunisia where we should end our trip.
